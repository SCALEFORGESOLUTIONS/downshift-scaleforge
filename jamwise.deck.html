Optional · category research

Why music, briefly.

Song aids recall

Text set to music is recalled better than the same text spoken (Wallace, 1994). Later work suggests presentation rate carries part of that effect (Kilgour et al., 2000) — the finding is real, and it is contested.

Retrieval beats review

Being made to produce an answer reliably outperforms rereading it (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006). This one is not contested.

JamWise sits on both: the song for encoding, the games for retrieval. That is a design rationale — not a claim about student outcomes. We don't have JamWise-specific efficacy data, and we won't pretend otherwise.
