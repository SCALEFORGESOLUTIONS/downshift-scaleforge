// PROVENANCE · the spine

The tool that abstains
is the one you can quote.

In a category whose top complaint is signal-to-noise, and a moment when every model will confidently invent a competitor fact, refusing to make things up is the sharpest wedge we have. Every claim in a report is either sourced or withheld.

What a claim carries

A source, a date, and a link to the live surface that proves it — the pricing page, the changelog, the ad library, the job post. Click through and check anything.

LIVE · rivalize.ai/product · "every move links back to where we found it"

What happens when it cannot

The cell abstains. It does not guess, and it does not render a fake zero. A number below threshold reads ? ABSTAIN with the console line that explains why.

PRECEDENT · sources below threshold → verdict withheld

We hold ourselves to it too. Rivalize's own momentum renders 1 / 100 with an early-signal caption and a banner saying it reflects pre-launch stage, not product quality. We did not round it up.
