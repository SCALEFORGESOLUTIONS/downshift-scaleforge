The difference

Most educational music stops when the song ends.

Ours is where the practice starts. Ten game modes turn one jam into ten different reps — and five of them use the jam's actual audio as the game.

Music

Real-time karaoke fill-in-the-blank. Miss a word and the music skips. Students aren't pulling vocabulary off a worksheet — they're recalling it against a beat that won't wait.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Is JamWise available yet?

Not quite. We're opening access in batches from the waitlist. Join and we'll email you when it's your turn.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan includes the full 250+ jam library, projector mode, assigning to classes, four game modes, and up to 35 students. No credit card, no trial clock.

Which grades and subjects?

Kindergarten through Grade 8, across five subjects — math, science, ELA, social studies, and social-emotional learning.

How are the jams aligned to standards?

Each jam is tagged to CCSS, NGSS, C3 or CASEL, and you can filter the library by standard. JamWise is a supplement to your instruction, not a replacement for it.

Can I see what students understood?

Yes. Game results come back per student and per standard, so gaps show up before the unit test does.

Is it safe for kids?

Students never sign up themselves — they join through your class. No ads, no tracking, no data sold, no social features. COPPA compliant.
