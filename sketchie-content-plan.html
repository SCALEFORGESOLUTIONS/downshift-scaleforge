The whole plan in one rule

Build for the forward, not the like.

The signals most consistently tied to reach beyond your own followers are watch time and sends. Likes and saves are disputed. So every video is written to be forwarded by one teacher to another, and anything that cannot be is treated differently.

Nobody forwards a feature tour.

So we are not doing

  • Themed weekdays. The slot comes first and the idea second, and it shows.
  • Topic pillars. "Maths, science, product" describes a catalogue, not a reason to follow.
  • A dated grid. Pin a post to 3 December and the plan expires on the 4th.

A fair warning on the research: it is almost all vendor and practitioner writing, not independent study. The structure is borrowed. The numbers should be ours.
